What are Voice Agents?

Voice Agents are intelligent AI-powered agents that can carry real phone conversations, in real time, with a natural voice.

They use advanced natural language processing (NLP) and state-of-the-art voice synthesis to understand context, respond intelligently and adapt the tone of the conversation. The result? A speaker that cannot be distinguished from a real person.

From handling inbound calls to automated outbound campaigns, these agents operate around the clock, without fatigue, without errors and at a fraction of the cost of a human team.
