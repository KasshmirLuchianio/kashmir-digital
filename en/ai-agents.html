Concept

What are AI Agents?

An AI agent is an autonomous software system that analyzes, decides, and acts without human intervention, tailored to your business needs.

Autonomy and adaptability

Unlike traditional rule-based automations, an AI agent learns from your data, adapts to new situations, and makes intelligent decisions in real time. Think of it as a digital employee that never gets tired.

Full integration

AI agents connect to your existing systems — CRM, ERP, email, e-commerce platforms — and operate simultaneously across multiple channels, centralizing information and executing tasks automatically.
